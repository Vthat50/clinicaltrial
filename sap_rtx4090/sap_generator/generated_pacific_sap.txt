2. INTRODUCTION
2.1 Study Background
[Protocol Section 3.1: "This is a Phase III, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multi-centre study assessing the efficacy and safety of MEDI4736 compared with placebo as sequential therapy"] The PACIFIC study evaluates durvalumab (MEDI4736) maintenance therapy following concurrent chemoradiation therapy in patients with locally advanced, unresectable non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) Stage III who have not progressed after definitive treatment.

2.2 Study Objectives
Primary Objective: [Protocol Section 2.1: "To assess the efficacy of MEDI4736 treatment compared with placebo in terms of overall survival (OS) and progression free survival (PFS)"]

Secondary Objectives: To further assess efficacy in terms of OS24, ORR, DoR, APF12, APF18, PFS2, and TTDM; to assess safety and tolerability; to evaluate pharmacokinetics and immunogenicity; and to assess quality of life measures.

2.3 Study Endpoints
Co-Primary Endpoints: [Protocol Section 11.1.3: Overall Survival and Progression-Free Survival per RECIST 1.1 as assessed by BICR]

Secondary Endpoints: OS24, ORR, DoR, APF12, APF18, PFS2, TTDM, safety and tolerability, pharmacokinetics, immunogenicity, and patient-reported outcomes.

3. STUDY DESIGN
3.1 Overall Design
[Protocol Section 3.1: "This is a Phase III, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multi-centre study"] The study employs a parallel-group design with 2:1 randomization (durvalumab:placebo). [Protocol Section 12.3: "Approximately 702 patients will be randomised 2:1 to MEDI4736 or placebo"]

3.2 Treatment Arms
Treatment Arms:

MEDI4736 (Durvalumab): [Protocol Section 3.1: "10 mg/kg every 2 weeks iv for up to 12 months"] (N=468)
Placebo: [Protocol Section 3.1: "matching placebo for infusion Q2W iv for up to 12 months"] (N=234)
Stratification Factors: [Protocol Section 5.2.1: "age at randomisation (<65 years versus ≥65 years of age), sex (male versus female), and smoking history (smoker versus non-smoker)"]

4. SAMPLE SIZE & POWER
4.1 Primary Endpoint Sample Size
[Protocol Section 12.3: "Approximately 702 patients will be randomised 2:1 to MEDI4736 or placebo"] Total enrollment of 702 patients randomized 2:1.

PFS Analysis: [Protocol Section 12.3: "If the true PFS HR is 0.67, the study will provide at least 95% power"] Based on 458 PFS events (65% maturity) with 95% power to detect HR=0.67 at 2.5% two-sided significance level.

OS Analysis: [Protocol Section 12.3: "If the true OS HR is 0.73, this number of death events will provide at least 85% power"] Based on 491 OS events (70% maturity) with 85% power to detect HR=0.73 at 2.5% two-sided significance level.

4.2 Assumptions
[Protocol Section 12.3: "this translates to a 5-month benefit in median PFS over 10 months on placebo"] Assumes median PFS of 10 months on placebo, 15 months on durvalumab.

[Protocol Section 12.3: "this translates to an 8-month benefit in median OS over 22 months on placebo"] Assumes median OS of 22 months on placebo, 30 months on durvalumab.

4.3 Power Calculation Details
PFS: 95% power for HR=0.67 with 458 events. OS: 85% power for HR=0.73 with 491 events. Alpha level of 2.5% (two-sided) allocated to each co-primary endpoint.

5. ANALYSIS POPULATIONS
5.1 Intent-to-Treat (ITT) Population
[Protocol Section 12.1.1: "The primary statistical analysis of the efficacy of MEDI4736 versus placebo will include all randomised patients and will compare the treatment arms on the basis of randomised treatment, regardless of the treatment actually received"]

5.2 Safety Population
[Protocol Section 12.1.2: "All patients who received at least one dose of randomised study drug"]

5.3 Per-Protocol Population
Subset of ITT population excluding patients with major protocol deviations.

6. ENDPOINTS & ESTIMANDS
6.1 Primary Endpoint(s)
Overall Survival (OS): [Protocol Section 11.1.3.1: "OS is defined as the time from the date of randomisation until death due to any cause"]

Progression-Free Survival (PFS): [Protocol Section 11.1.3.2: "PFS (per RECIST 1.1 as assessed by BICR)"]

6.4 Estimand Framework
[ICH E9(R1) Section 5.2 - Estimand Framework] Each endpoint follows ICH E9(R1) estimand framework with components:

Population: ITT population
Treatment: Durvalumab vs Placebo
Endpoint: Time-to-event or binary response
Intercurrent Events: New anticancer therapy, treatment discontinuation
Population-level Summary: Hazard ratio or response rate difference

7. STATISTICAL METHODS
7.1 General Considerations
All statistical tests are two-sided unless otherwise specified. The significance level is 2.5% for each co-primary endpoint.

Primary Analysis Method: [Protocol Section 12.2.2.1: "OS will be analysed using a stratified log-rank test"] Stratified log-rank test for time-to-event endpoints.

7.2 Time-to-Event Analysis
Kaplan-Meier method for survival curve estimation. Stratified log-rank test for hypothesis testing. Cox proportional hazards model for hazard ratio estimation.

9. MISSING DATA HANDLING
9.1 Missing Data Conventions
Missing assessments will be handled according to protocol-specified censoring rules. No imputation will be performed for time-to-event endpoints.

10. SENSITIVITY ANALYSES
10.1 Primary Endpoint Sensitivity Analyses
Multiple sensitivity analyses will be performed including PFS Investigator Assessment, Alternative Censoring Rules, Unstratified Analysis, Per-Protocol Population.

11. SUBGROUP ANALYSES
11.1 Pre-specified Subgroups
Age at randomisation (<65 years vs ≥65 years), Sex (Male vs Female), Smoking history (Smoker vs Non-smoker), ECOG Performance Status, Disease Stage, Histology.

12. SAFETY ANALYSIS
12.1 Adverse Events
Safety analysis using CTCAE v4.03 grading. Treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) defined as events with onset on or after first dose through 28 days after last dose.

13. INTERIM ANALYSIS
13.1 Timing and Information Fractions
The study includes three planned interim analyses at PFS Interim (367 events), First OS Interim (285 deaths), Second OS Interim (393 deaths).

13.2 Alpha Spending Function
Lan-DeMets alpha spending function approximating O'Brien-Fleming boundaries.

13.5 DMC/DSMB Charter Reference
Independent Data Monitoring Committee (IDMC) reviews all interim analyses per separate IDMC Charter.
