PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Side Hop
Frame: 
Method: Change in hop performance on the injured leg from baseline to 12 weeks, expressed in number of hops completed
Measure: Anterior Cruciate Ligament Return to Sport After Injury Scale
Frame: 
Method: 12 question self-reported outcome scale measuring readiness to return to sport. Scale ranges from 0-10 for each question. Scores summed from 0 (worst) - 100 (best).
