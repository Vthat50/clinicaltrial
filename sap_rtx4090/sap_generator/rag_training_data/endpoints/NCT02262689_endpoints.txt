PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Change from baseline of PASP under hypoxic conditions
Frame: 
Method: PASP is an indicator of cardiac hemodynamic status and is non-invasively estimated by echocardiography. Change from baseline in PASP under hypoxic conditions following 7 days of GSK2256294 15 milligrams (mg) once daily. Change from baseline was calculated as the Day 7 value minus the Day1 value.
