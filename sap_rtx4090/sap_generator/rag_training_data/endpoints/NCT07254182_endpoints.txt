8.1 Primary endpoint 
• Minimum dorsal hand Tsk during the 30‑minute rewarming window (IV–X) measured by IRT 
(ROI mean); between‑group difference (ice vs control). 
 
8.2 Key secondary endpoints 
• Dorsal hand Tsk at matched time points (I, IV–X) by IRT and RT; between‑group differences and 
IRT–RT offsets. 
• MDT change (post‑cooling minus baseline; IV–I). 
• VDT change (IV–I). 
• Subjective scales: TSS (−4 very cold … +4 very hot), CS (1 comfortable … 5 extremely 
uncomfortable), VAS pain (0–100 mm). Outcomes include peak during cooling (II/III) and 
immediate post‑cooling (IV).
