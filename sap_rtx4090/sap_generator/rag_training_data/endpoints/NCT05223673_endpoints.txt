1.2. Study design   
1.2.1. 
Study plan  
This is a randomised, open label, multi-centre, two-arm Phase 3 study to evaluate 
futuximab/modotuximab in combination with trifluridine/tipiracil versus trifluridine/tipiracil 
monotherapy in participants ≥ 18 years of age with KRAS/NRAS and BRAF WT mCRC who 
were previously treated by chemotherapy (including oxaliplatin, irinotecan and 5-fluorouracil, 
anti-VEGF agents) and with anti-EGFR mAb therapy for ≥ 16 weeks.
