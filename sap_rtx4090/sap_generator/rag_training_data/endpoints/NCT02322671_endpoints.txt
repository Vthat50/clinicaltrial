PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Composite of plasma pharmacokinetic [PK] parameters of montelukast sodium
Frame: 
Method: Plasma PK parameters includes Maximum observed concentration (Cmax), Area under the concentration-time curve from time zero (pre-dose) extrapolated to infinite\[inf\] time (AUC\[0-inf\]) and Area under the concentration-time curve from time zero (pre-dose) to last time of quantifiable concentration within a subject across all treatments (AUC\[0-t\]).
