PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Number of Participants With Overall Preference for Nasal Spray Assessed by Preference Questionnaire.
Frame: 
Method: An overall preference questionnaire (OPQ) was used to evaluate participants' preference for nasal spray therapy for the given treatments. OPQ allows the responder three options, based on products attributes, i.e. preference for product 1; preference for product 2 and no preference. The OPQ was completed by each participant approximately 4 minutes after administration of the second treatment in Period 2.
