PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Change From Baseline in Trough Morning Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 Second (FEV1) at Day 85
Frame: 
Method: Pulmonary function was measured by FEV1, defined as the maximal amount of air that can be forcefully exhaled in one second. The trough FEV1 is defined as morning prebronchodilator and predose (12 hours after the last evening dose Day 84). Trough FEV1 was measured electronically by spirometer in the morning, before using the bronchodilator and predose, at Week 12 (Day 85) of each Treatment Period.
