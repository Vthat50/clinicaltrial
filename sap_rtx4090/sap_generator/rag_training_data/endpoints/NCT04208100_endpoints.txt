Primary Endpoint: 
Percentage of pigmentary clearance by each wavelength in standard photographs from the 30-
day visit post the last treatment in Phase I of the study using a score of 0-4, representing poor 0-
24%, fair 25-49%, good 50-74%, excellent 75-95%, and complete 95%+ improvement was given at 
the day 30 follow-up visit post the last treatment as determined by three blinded physician 
evaluators comparing the baseline vs 30-day follow-up visit photographs. 
Secondary Endpoints: 
1. The Principal Investigator, sub-investigator or qualified clinician delegated by the 
principal investigator, will complete a Clinician Global Aesthetic Improvement Scale 
assessing overall aesthetic improvement by wavelength at the 30-day visit following the 
last treatment visit for Phase I of the study.  
2. The subject will complete a SGAIS assessing overall aesthetic improvement by 
wavelength at the 30-day visit following the last treatment visit for Phase I of the study. 
3. The subject will complete a Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire by wavelength at the 30-
day visit following the last treatment visit for Phase I of the study.  
4. During all study treatments, the subject’s pain levels will be monitored using a validated 
Numeric Rating Scale. The maximum and average pain score will be recorded by 
wavelength. 
Safety Variables: 
 
Prior to test spot exposure, the subject’s medical history will be reviewed, a urine pregnancy 
test will be performed (if applicable), and visual skin examination will be conducted to ensure 
the subject qualifies for the study and that the exposure area is free of open wounds.
