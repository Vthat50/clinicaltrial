PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Number of Participants With First Occurrence of Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events (MACE) Through Week 12
Frame: 
Method: The primary efficacy endpoint is the composite measure of adjudicated MACE that includes the time to first occurrence of CV death (death due to a cardiovascular cause), MI or SRI-UR (Severe Recurrent Ischemia requiring Urgent coronary artery Revascularization). Death for which the Clinical Events Committee (CEC) or investigator were unable to establish cause were analyzed as CV deaths.
