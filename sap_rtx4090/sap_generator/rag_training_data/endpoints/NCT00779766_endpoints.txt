PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Number of Subjects With Histopathologically-confirmed Cervical Intraepithelial Neoplasia (CIN1+) and/or Persistent Infection (6 Month+ Definition) Associated With Human Papillomavirus (HPV)-16 and/or HPV-18 at Month 24
Frame: 
Method: CIN1+ = CIN 1, 2, and 3, low/high-grade cervical glandular intraepithelial neoplasia (L/HCGIN), adenocarcinoma in-situ (AIS) or invasive cervical cancer. Persistent infection (6-month+ definition) = at least 2 positive HPV deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) polymerase chain reaction (PCR) assays for the same viral genotype with no negative DNA sample between the 2 positive DNA samples, over an interval of approximately 6 months. The analyses were done on subjects HPV DNA negative at baseline (Month 0)
