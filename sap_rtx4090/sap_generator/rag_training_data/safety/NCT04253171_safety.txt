Safety Endpoints
1. major adverse cardiac events and its components within one year:
-1a Cardiac death at or before one year after the index procedure
-1b Any myocardial infarction at or before one year after the index procedure
-1c Stroke at or before one year after the index procedure
2. In-hospital procedure events
-2a Periprocedural myocardial infarction
-2b Coronary dissection (flow-limiting or beyond intended by lesion preparation)
-2c Coronary perforation/rupture
