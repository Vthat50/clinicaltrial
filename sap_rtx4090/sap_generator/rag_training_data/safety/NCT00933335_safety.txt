Adverse Event (AE)
  Time Frame: N/A
  Description: An AE was defined as any untoward medical occurrence in a participant administered a pharmaceutical product and does not necessarily have to have a causal relationship (association) with this treatment. Therefore, an AE was any unfavorable and unintended sign, symptom, or disease temporally associated with the use of a medicinal product, whether or not it was considered to be related to the medicinal product.
