  Outcome: Number of Subjects With Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)
  Time Frame: N/A
  Description: A SAE was defined as any untoward medical occurrence that: resulted in death, was life threatening, required hospitalization or prolongation of hospitalization, resulted in disability/incapacity or was a congenital anomaly/birth defect in the offspring of a study subject. Any was defined as an occurrence of an SAE, regardless its relationship to vaccination.
