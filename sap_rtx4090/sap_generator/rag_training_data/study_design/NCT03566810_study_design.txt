Purpose of the Statistical Analysis Plan
The purpose of this Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) is to document technical and detailed 
specifications for the final analysis of data collected for protocol MS200084_0013, Version 1.0.
Results of the analyses described in this SAP will be included in the Clinical Study Report (CSR). 
Additionally, the planned analyses identified in this SAP will be included in regulatory 
submissions or future manuscripts. Any post-hoc, or unplanned analyses performed to provide 
results for inclusion in the CSR but not identified in this prospective SAP will be clearly identified 
in the CSR.
