Purpose of the Statistical Analysis Plan
The purpose of this statistical analysis plan (SAP) is to document technical and detailed 
specifications for the interim and final analysis of data collected for protocol EMR 100070-004.
Results of the analyses described in this SAP will be included in the Clinical Study Report (CSR).
Additionally, the planned analyses identified in this SAP will be included in regulatory 
submissions or future manuscripts. Any post-hoc, or unplanned analyses performed to provide 
results for inclusion in the CSR but not identified in this prospective SAP will be clearly identified 
in the CSR.
The SAP is based upon section 8 (Statistics) of the trial protocol and protocol amendments and is 
prepared in compliance with International Conference on Harmonization (ICH) E9. The first
version (version 1.0) focuses on the detailed description of the primary and key secondary 
endpoints analysis and key safety endpoints analysis. Version 2.0 and version 3.0 were created 
based on the protocol version 5.0 dated 29 November 2016. Additional updates in version 2.0 and
v3.0 are specified in Section 5.1, Changes to Previous Version.
