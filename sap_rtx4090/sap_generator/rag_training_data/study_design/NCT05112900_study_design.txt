KEY STAKEHOLDERS
The Food and Drug Administration’s published guidance on estimands [2], describes the importance of aligning
the clinical trial estimand (quantity of interest) with the objectives of key stakeholders. Key stakeholders included
the Utah Department of Health, the Granite and Salt Lake City School Districts, participating charter schoools,
and the Principal Investigators of the grant proposal (Wu and Wetter).
