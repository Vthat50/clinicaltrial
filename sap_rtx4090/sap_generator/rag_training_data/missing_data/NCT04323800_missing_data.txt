Randomization

random treatment assignments using a documented process. The randomization schedule will be designed to 
yield an expected allocation ratio of 1:1 for HCP + control plasma. Assignments will be stratified by clinical site 
and schedules will employ permuted block designs, with block sizes to be determined and documented at the
