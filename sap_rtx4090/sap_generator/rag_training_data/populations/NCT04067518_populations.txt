Demographic data and other baseline characteristics will be summarized by part of study and overall for the SAF, 
IMAS and PKAS. Same summarization will be done for the FAS (Part 2 only). 
The following demographic and other baseline characteristics will be reported for this study: 
 
Age (years) at informed consent 
 
Category of age (years): “< 10”, “>= 10” 
 
Sex: “Female”, “Male” 
 
Race: “Asian” 
 
Weight (kg) at baseline visit 
 
Height (cm) at baseline visit 
 
Body surface area (BSA) (m2) at baseline visit
