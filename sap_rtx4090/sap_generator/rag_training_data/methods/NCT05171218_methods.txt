Statistical Analysis Plan 
 
For each participant the post-experimental treatment STICSA, PANAS and SAM Valence and arousal scores will be 
subtracted from the pre-experimental treatment scores. Shapiro-Wilks normality and homogeneity of variances 
tests will be done on the STICSA, PANAS and SAM datasets, and homogeneity of covariances tests will be done on 
music preferences, and personality type datasets to determine if assumptions of the Multivariate Analysis of 
Covariance (MANCOVA) are satisfied. If these assumptions are satisfied, a MANCOVA over the factor of experimental
