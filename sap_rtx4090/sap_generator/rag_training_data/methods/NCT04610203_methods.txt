Statistical analysis  
Data will be analysed using the IBM Statistical Package for the Social Sciences (SPSS, 
version 24).  Data will be presented as mean, standard deviation (SD) and standard error 
of the mean (SEM) values. The blood glucose, plasma insulin, GLP-1 and GIP iAUC (at 60, 
90, 120 and 180 min) will be calculated geometrically by applying the trapezoid rule, 
according to the ISO standards.  Prior to statistical analysis, the normality of the data will 
be tested using the Shapiro-Wilks statistic.  Parametric or non-parametric tests will be 
used as appropriate to compare the following between the products: 
• Phase I 
o blood glucose/insulin concentrations 
o peak blood glucose/insulin value and time of the blood glucose/insulin peak 
o glucose/insulin iAUC (at 60, 90, 120 and 180 min)  
• Phase II 
o GLP-1 and GIP concentrations 
o GLP-1 and GIP iAUC (at 60, 90, 120 and 180 min)  
 
Statistical significance will be set at P <0.05.
