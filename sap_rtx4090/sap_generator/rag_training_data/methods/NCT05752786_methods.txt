Sample size rationale 
The sample size was determined based on a power analysis (GPower v3.1) to detect a small 
effect size of f=0.10 for a within-between interaction in a mixed model with 3 repeated 
measures. A total sample size of 164 participants was necessary to reach 0.80 power at 0.05 
alpha. Considering an attrition rate in between 15 and 20%, we will aim to recruit 200 
university students willing to take part in the challenge.
