Sample: We will create an EMR-based cohort of adult patients age > 21 scheduled to 
receive a TAVR procedure surgery at Yale-New Haven Hospital under conscious sedation 
between December 2014 and June 2016. The Multicenter Perioperative Outcomes Group 
(MPOG) local data repository in conjunction with subsequent chart review will be used for 
cohort identification. 
 
Data available: Data on patients will include: demographics, vital signs, medications, 
anesthesia notes, laboratory values, clinician and health care encounter information 
(including, provider type, location, date time), as well as diagnostic codes, procedure codes, 
clinic notes, and pharmacy records associated with that encounter.
