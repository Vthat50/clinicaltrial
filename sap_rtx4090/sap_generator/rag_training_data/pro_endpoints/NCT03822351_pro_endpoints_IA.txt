Early Go criteria are met if it is predicted that there is a high probability of reaching a Go 
decision upon full randomization of 60 subjects given the existing observed data (i.e., 
predictive probability of a Go decision > 95%). If Go criteria are met, randomization may 
continue, and planning activities for further development of the durvalumab/novel agent 
combination may be triggered.
