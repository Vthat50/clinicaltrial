### Key features:
- Central AND Local review for all tumor endpoints
- Both ITT and PP populations for efficacy
- Induction period (primary) AND whole study period (secondary) efficacy
- Tipping point analysis for equivalence robustness
- 4 study periods for safety: Whole, Induction, Maintenance, Follow-up
- Per-cycle drug exposure for all 3 drugs (study drug + 2 backbone)
- 11 AESIs (bevacizumab-specific)
- ADA-stratified safety analysis
- Prior cancer therapy broken down by modality
- Salvage/subsequent therapy tracking
