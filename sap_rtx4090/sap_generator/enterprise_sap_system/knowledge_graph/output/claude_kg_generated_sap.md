## 2. STUDY OBJECTIVES AND ENDPOINTS

### Primary Objectives and Endpoints

1. **Safety Assessment (Part 1 - Safety Run-in)**
   - **Endpoint:** Percentage of Participants Experiencing Dose Limiting Toxicities (DLTs) in Safety Run-in (Part 1)
   - **Time Frame:** From the first dose date up to 5 weeks
   - **Definition:** A DLT was defined as a ≥ Grade 3 AE that was assessed as related to either magrolimab or avelumab that occurred during the 5-week DLT assessment period with protocol-defined allowed exceptions
   - *Source: PRIMARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

2. **Safety Assessment (Overall Study)**
   - **Endpoint:** Percentage of Participants Experiencing Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs)
   - **Time Frame:** First dose date up to last dose plus 30 days (maximum treatment duration 18.3 months)
   - **Definition:** Treatment-emergent AEs were defined as those AEs that worsened or occurred during or after a participant's first dose of any study treatment and those existing AEs that worsened during the study and within 30 days after the last administration
   - *Source: PRIMARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

3. **Efficacy Assessment (Part 2 - Ovarian Cancer Expansion)**
   - **Endpoint:** Percentage of Participants With Objective Response (ORR) Assessed by RECIST Version 1.1 in Participants With Ovarian Cancer
   - **Time Frame:** From screening until 26.2 months
   - **Definition:** Objective response was defined as the percentage of participants with objective response which consisted of complete response (CR)+ partial response (PR) determined by RECIST v 1.1
   - *Source: PRIMARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

### Secondary Objectives and Endpoints

1. **Dose Determination**
   - **Endpoint:** Recommended Phase 2 Dose and Schedule (RP2DS) of Magrolimab in Combination With Avelumab
   - **Definition:** The RP2DS was the dose of magrolimab in combination with avelumab with DLT rate less than 33% in at least 6 evaluable participants in Part 1
   - *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

2. **Pharmacokinetics**
   - **Endpoint:** Serum Concentrations of Magrolimab - Safety Run-in (Part 1)
   - **Definition:** Pharmacokinetic assessment of magrolimab serum concentrations
   - *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

3. **Immunogenicity**
   - **Endpoint:** Percentage of Participants With Transient Anti-Drug Antibody to Magrolimab - Safety Run-in (Part 1)
   - **Definition:** Assessment of immunogenicity of magrolimab
   - *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

4. **Alternative Efficacy Assessment**
   - **Endpoint:** Percentage of Participants With Objective Response According to Gynecologic Cancer InterGroup (GCIG) Criteria in Ovarian Cancer
   - **Definition:** Objective response was defined as participants with a CR or a PR as assessed per GCIG criteria using both RECIST and cancer antigen 125 (CA-125) criteria
   - *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

5. **Duration of Response**
   - **Endpoint:** Duration of Response (DOR) as Per GCIG Criteria in Participants With Ovarian Cancer
   - **Definition:** DOR: time from initial response (CR or PR) until disease progression
   - *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

6. **Survival Endpoints**
   - **Endpoint:** Progression-free Survival (PFS) in Participants With Ovarian Cancer
   - **Definition:** PFS was defined as the duration of time from dose initiation to the first date of objectively documented disease progression per RECIST 1.1 or death, whichever occurred at first
   - *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

   - **Endpoint:** Overall Survival (OS) in Participants With Ovarian Cancer
   - **Definition:** OS was defined as the duration of time from dose initiation to the date of death due to any cause
   - *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

7. **Biomarker Assessment**
   - **Endpoint:** Percent Change of Immune Cells by Immunohistochemistry in Participants With Ovarian Cancer
   - **Definition:** Paired tumor biopsies analyzed by CD68 immunohistochemistry staining to evaluate the impact of magrolimab in combination with avelumab on macrophage frequency in the tumor microenvironment
   - *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

## 3. STUDY DESIGN

**Study Design:** Phase 1b, non-randomized, sequential assignment, open-label study

**Study Structure:**
- **Part 1:** Safety Run-in with solid tumor participants
- **Part 2:** Ovarian cancer expansion cohort

**Treatment Arms:**
1. **Arm 1:** Magrolimab 30 mg/kg + Avelumab 800 mg (Part 1, Safety Run-in)
2. **Arm 2:** Magrolimab 45 mg/kg + Avelumab 800 mg (Part 1, Safety Run-in)
3. **Arm 3:** Magrolimab 45 mg/kg + Avelumab 800 mg (Part 2, Ovarian Cancer Expansion)

**Dosing Schedule:**
- **Priming dose:** 1 mg/kg magrolimab IV on Day 1 of Cycle 1
- **Maintenance dose:** 30 mg/kg or 45 mg/kg magrolimab IV per arm assignment
- **Cycle Length:** Cycle 1 = 35 days; Cycles 2+ = 28 days
- **Avelumab:** 800 mg IV administered 1 hour prior to magrolimab on combination days

**Safety Analysis Set (SAF):**

**DLT-Evaluable Set:**
- Participants in Part 1 who received study treatment and completed the 5-week DLT assessment period or experienced a DLT

**Efficacy Analysis Set:**
- All participants in Part 2 (ovarian cancer cohort) who received at least one dose of study treatment and had at least one post-baseline tumor assessment

**Pharmacokinetic Analysis Set:**
- All participants who received at least one dose of magrolimab and had at least one evaluable PK sample

## 5. STATISTICAL METHODS

### 5.1 General Principles [INFERRED]
- **Significance Level:** α = 0.05 (two-sided) for hypothesis testing
- **Software:** [To be specified]
- **Missing Data:** See Section 8

### 5.2 Safety Analyses

**DLT Analysis:**
- **Method:** Descriptive statistics
- **Population:** DLT-Evaluable Set
- **Endpoint:** Percentage of participants experiencing DLTs within 5-week assessment period
- **Criterion:** DLT rate < 33% in at least 6 evaluable participants for RP2DS determination
- *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

**TEAE Analysis:**
- **Method:** Descriptive statistics by treatment arm
- **Population:** Safety Analysis Set
- **Summaries:** Frequency and percentage of participants with TEAEs by System Organ Class and Preferred Term

### 5.3 Efficacy Analyses

**Objective Response Rate (ORR) - RECIST v1.1:**
- **Method:** Point estimate with 95% confidence interval [INFERRED]
- **Population:** Efficacy Analysis Set (ovarian cancer cohort)
- **Assessment:** Response Evaluation Criteria In Solid Tumors version 1.1
- *Source: PRIMARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

**Objective Response Rate - GCIG Criteria:**
- **Method:** Point estimate with 95% confidence interval [INFERRED]
- **Population:** Efficacy Analysis Set (ovarian cancer cohort)
- **Assessment:** Gynecologic Cancer InterGroup criteria using both RECIST and CA-125 criteria
- *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section, doc:7e33bd24*

**Time-to-Event Analyses:**
- **Method:** Kaplan-Meier estimates with 95% confidence intervals
- **Endpoints:** PFS, OS, DOR
- **Population:** Efficacy Analysis Set (ovarian cancer cohort)
- **Censoring Rules:**
  - PFS: Participants without progression or death censored at last tumor assessment
  - OS: Participants alive censored at last known alive date
  - DOR: Participants with ongoing response censored at last tumor assessment
- *Source: SECONDARY OUTCOMES section mentions Kaplan-Meier method, doc:7e33bd24*

## 8. MISSING DATA HANDLING

### 8.1 General Principles [INFERRED]
- Missing data patterns will be characterized and summarized
- Primary analysis based on observed data (complete case analysis)
- Sensitivity analyses to assess impact of missing data assumptions

### 8.2 Specific Endpoints

**Safety Data:**
- Participants included in safety summaries if they received at least one dose
- Missing safety data assumed to represent absence of events

**Efficacy Data:**
- Missing tumor assessments handled according to RECIST v1.1 guidelines
- Progressive disease assigned for missed assessments in certain circumstances
- Censoring rules applied for time-to-event endpoints as specified in Section 5.3

**PK Data:**
- Concentrations below quantification limit handled according to standard PK practices
- Missing PK samples excluded from concentration summaries
