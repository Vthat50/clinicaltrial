# STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN

**Study Title:** Retreatment Study of Patients With Non-Hodgkin's Lymphoma Who Have Previously Responded to Iodine-131 Anti B1 Antibody

**Protocol Number:** NCT00938041  
**Sponsor:** GlaxoSmithKline  
**Phase:** Phase 2

---

## 1. STUDY OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Study Design
- **Study Type:** Single-arm, open-label, Phase 2 interventional study
- **Study Population:** Patients with non-Hodgkin's B-cell lymphoma who previously responded to Iodine-131 Anti-B1 Antibody therapy with duration of response ≥3 months
- **Sample Size:** 32 patients
- **Treatment:** Tositumomab and Iodine I 131 Tositumomab retreatment

### 1.2 Study Period
- **Start Date:** April 1998
- **Completion Date:** June 2013
- **Follow-up Duration:** Every 6 months until disease progression, death, or for 2 years following the dosimetric dose

---

## 2. STUDY OBJECTIVES AND ENDPOINTS

### 2.1 Primary Objectives
1. To determine the complete response rate and confirmed complete response rate
2. To determine the duration of response for all confirmed responders (CR + CCR + PR)
3. To evaluate progression-free survival
4. To evaluate time to treatment failure
5. To evaluate overall survival
6. To assess safety (serious adverse events and adverse events)

### 2.2 Endpoint Definitions

**Complete Response (CR):** Complete resolution of all disease-related radiological abnormalities and disappearance of all signs and symptoms related to the disease.

**Clinical Complete Response (CCR):** Complete resolution of all disease-related symptoms, but residual foci thought to be residual scar tissue are present (≤2 cm diameter by radiographic evaluation or ≤1 cm diameter by physical examination).

**Partial Response (PR):** ≥50% reduction in the sum of the products of the longest perpendicular diameters of all measurable lesions, with no new lesions.

**Progressive Disease (PD):** ≥25% increase from the nadir value of the sum of the products of the longest perpendicular diameters of all measurable lesions or appearance of any new lesion.

**Duration of Response:** Time from first documented response to first documented progression (for CR, CCR, or PR patients).

**Progression-Free Survival:** Time from start of retreatment (dosimetric dose) to first documented progression or death.

**Time to Treatment Failure:** Time from dosimetric dose to first occurrence of treatment withdrawal, decision to receive additional therapy, study withdrawal, disease progression, or death.

**Overall Survival:** Time from start of retreatment (dosimetric dose) to death from any cause.

## 3. STUDY POPULATIONS

### 3.1 Analysis Populations
- **Intent-to-Treat (ITT) Population:** All enrolled patients who received the dosimetric dose
- **Safety Population:** All patients who received any study drug
- **Efficacy-Evaluable Population:** All patients who received treatment and had at least one post-baseline efficacy assessment

**Response Rate Analysis:**
- Complete response rate and confirmed complete response rate will be calculated as proportions with 95% confidence intervals using exact binomial methods
- Overall response rate (CR + CCR + PR) will be calculated similarly

**Time-to-Event Analyses:**
- Duration of response, progression-free survival, time to treatment failure, and overall survival will be analyzed using Kaplan-Meier methodology
- Median times with 95% confidence intervals will be estimated
