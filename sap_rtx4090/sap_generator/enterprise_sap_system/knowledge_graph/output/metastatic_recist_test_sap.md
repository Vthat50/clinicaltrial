# Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP)

**Study Title:** A Phase II Study of the c-MET RTK Inhibitor XL880 in Subjects With Papillary Renal-Cell Carcinoma

**Protocol Number:** NCT00726323  
**Sponsor:** GlaxoSmithKline

---

## 1. STUDY OBJECTIVES

### 1.1 Primary Objective
To evaluate the overall response rate (ORR) of foretinib in subjects with papillary renal-cell carcinoma.

### 1.2 Secondary Objectives
- To evaluate disease stabilization rate
- To assess progression-free survival (PFS)
- To determine time to response (TTR)
- To assess duration of response (DOR)
- To evaluate duration of stable disease (SD)

---

## 2. STUDY DESIGN

This is a Phase II, non-randomized, open-label, parallel assignment study evaluating two dosing regimens of foretinib in subjects with papillary renal-cell carcinoma:
- **Arm 1:** 240 mg foretinib on a 5 day on/9 day off regimen every 14 days
- **Arm 2:** 80 mg foretinib on a daily dosing regimen

**Target Enrollment:** 74 subjects

---

## 3. ENDPOINTS

### 3.1 Primary Endpoint
**Overall Response Rate (ORR):** Percentage of participants with confirmed complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) as best overall response, assessed by investigator using RECIST version 1.0 criteria.

### 3.2 Secondary Endpoints
1. **Disease Stabilization Rate:** Percentage of participants with CR, PR, or stable disease (SD) as best overall response
2. **Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** Time from first dose to disease progression or death from any cause
3. **Time to Response (TTR):** Time from first dose to first documented CR or PR
4. **Duration of Response (DOR):** Time from first documented CR or PR to disease progression or death
5. **Duration of Stable Disease:** Time from first documented SD to disease progression or death (for patients with SD as best response)

---

## 4. ANALYSIS POPULATIONS

### 4.1 Intent-to-Treat (ITT) Population
All enrolled subjects who received at least one dose of study drug.

### 4.2 Efficacy Evaluable Population
All subjects in the ITT population who have measurable disease at baseline and at least one post-baseline tumor assessment.

### 4.3 Safety Population
All enrolled subjects who received at least one dose of study drug.

---

## 5. STATISTICAL METHODS

### 5.1 General Considerations
- All statistical tests will be two-sided with α = 0.05
- No formal hypothesis testing is planned for this Phase II study
- Missing data will not be imputed for efficacy analyses
- Analyses will be performed for the overall population and by treatment arm

### 5.2 Primary Efficacy Analysis

**Overall Response Rate:**
- ORR will be calculated as the number of subjects with confirmed CR or PR divided by the number of evaluable subjects
- 95% confidence intervals will be calculated using the exact binomial method (Clopper-Pearson)
- Analysis will be performed on the Efficacy Evaluable Population
- Best overall response will be summarized by treatment arm using frequency and percentages

### 5.3 Secondary Efficacy Analyses

**Disease Stabilization Rate:**
- Calculated as the number of subjects with CR, PR, or SD divided by the number of evaluable subjects
- 95% confidence intervals will be calculated using the exact binomial method

**Time-to-Event Endpoints (PFS, TTR, DOR, Duration of SD):**
- Kaplan-Meier method will be used to estimate survival curves
- Median time with 95% confidence intervals will be calculated
- Censoring rules:
  - PFS: Censored at last tumor assessment date for subjects without progression or death
  - TTR: Only calculated for responders (CR/PR); no censoring applicable
  - DOR: Censored at last tumor assessment date for responders without progression or death
  - Duration of SD: Censored at last tumor assessment date for subjects with SD who have not progressed or died

### 5.4 Tumor Response Analysis

**Best Overall Response Summary:**
- Frequency and percentage of subjects by response category (CR, PR, SD, PD, Not Evaluable)
- Waterfall plot of best percentage change from baseline in target lesion measurements
- Spider plot showing individual subject tumor size changes over time

**Response Confirmation:**
- All responses (CR/PR) must be confirmed by repeat assessment ≥4 weeks after initial documentation per RECIST 1.0

---

## 6. BASELINE AND DEMOGRAPHIC ANALYSES

### 6.1 Subject Disposition
- Subject enrollment and disposition will be summarized using frequency counts and percentages
- Reasons for discontinuation will be tabulated by treatment arm

### 6.2 Baseline Characteristics
Baseline demographics and disease characteristics will be summarized by treatment arm:
- Age (continuous and categorical: <65, ≥65 years)
- Sex
- Race/ethnicity
- ECOG performance status
- Prior anticancer therapy
- Disease stage
- Number and location of metastatic sites
- Target lesion characteristics

### 6.3 Treatment Exposure
- Duration of treatment
- Relative dose intensity
- Dose modifications and reasons

---

## 7. SAFETY ANALYSES

Safety analyses will be performed on the Safety Population and summarized by treatment arm:
- Adverse events by system organ class and preferred term (MedDRA coding)
- Serious adverse events
- Adverse events leading to dose modification or discontinuation
- Deaths and causes of death
- Laboratory abnormalities (shift tables from baseline)

---

## 8. INTERIM ANALYSES

No formal interim analyses are planned. Ongoing safety monitoring will be conducted by the Data Safety Monitoring Committee.

Any deviations from this SAP will be documented with appropriate justification before database lock.
