# Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP)

**Study Title:** GSK2302032A Antigen-Specific Cancer Immunotherapeutic as Adjuvant Therapy in Patients With Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

**NCT ID:** NCT01853878  
**Sponsor:** GlaxoSmithKline  
**Phase:** Phase 2

## 1. Study Design and Objectives

### 1.1 Study Design
- Phase 2, parallel-group, quadruple-masked, placebo-controlled study
- Target enrollment: 137 patients
- Population: Patients with radically resected NSCLC (pathological stage IA-T1b, IB, II or IIIA) with PRAME expression

### 1.2 Treatment Arms
- **Experimental Arm:** GSK2302032A (Recombinant PRAME protein combined with AS15 Adjuvant System)
- **Control Arm:** Placebo

### 1.3 Primary Objective
To evaluate the time to occurrence of any recurrence of disease in patients treated with GSK2302032A compared to placebo.

### 1.4 Primary Endpoint
Time to occurrence of any recurrence of disease (measured from Week 1 to Week 112)

## 2. Analysis Populations

### 2.1 Intent-to-Treat (ITT) Population
All randomized patients, analyzed according to randomized treatment assignment.

### 2.2 Per-Protocol (PP) Population
All patients in the ITT population who completed the study without major protocol violations.

### 2.3 Safety Population
All patients who received at least one dose of study treatment, analyzed according to actual treatment received.

## 3. Statistical Methods

### 3.1 General Considerations
- Primary analysis population: ITT
- Significance level: α = 0.05 (two-sided)
- Missing data: Patients will be censored at last known disease-free date

### 3.2 Study Discontinuation Impact
Due to premature study termination on August 24, 2016:
- Primary and secondary objectives were not assessed as planned
- All clinical and safety data collected were analyzed descriptively
- Median follow-up time was 10.3 months

## 4. Efficacy Analysis

### 4.1 Primary Efficacy Analysis

**Time to Occurrence of Any Recurrence of Disease**

**Primary Analysis Method:**
- Person-year rate calculation: Number of patients with recurrence (n) / Sum of follow-up period in years (T)
- Descriptive analysis due to early study termination

**Planned Analysis Methods (not performed due to early termination):**
- Kaplan-Meier survival curves by treatment group
- Cox proportional hazards regression model
- Hazard ratio with 95% confidence interval
- Log-rank test for treatment comparison

**Censoring Rules:**
- Patients without recurrence censored at last disease assessment date
- Patients who died without recurrence censored at death date
- Patients lost to follow-up censored at last known disease-free date

## 5. Baseline and Demographic Analysis

### 5.1 Patient Disposition
- Enrollment and randomization summary
- Study completion and discontinuation rates by treatment group
- Reasons for discontinuation

### 5.2 Baseline Demographics and Disease Characteristics
- Age (years)
- Sex
- ECOG performance status (0, 1, 2)
- Pathological stage (IA-T1b, IB, II, IIIA)
- PRAME expression status
- Type of surgical resection
- Time from surgery to randomization

### 5.3 Statistical Methods for Baseline Characteristics
- Continuous variables: descriptive statistics (n, mean, SD, median, min, max)
- Categorical variables: frequency counts and percentages

## 6. Safety Analysis

### 6.1 Safety Population
All patients who received at least one dose of study treatment.

### 6.2 Safety Endpoints
- Adverse events (AEs)
- Serious adverse events (SAEs)  
- Treatment discontinuations due to AEs
- Laboratory abnormalities
- Vital signs

### 6.3 Safety Analysis Methods
- Descriptive summaries by treatment group
- AEs coded using MedDRA terminology
- Severity graded according to CTCAE criteria
- Exposure-adjusted incidence rates where appropriate

## 7. Protocol Deviations and Missing Data

### 7.1 Protocol Deviations
Listing and summary of major protocol deviations by treatment group.

### 7.2 Missing Data Handling
- No imputation for primary endpoint due to descriptive analysis approach
- Missing baseline characteristics: no imputation, analyze observed data only

## 8. Interim Analysis

Not applicable due to early study termination.

## 9. Software

Statistical analyses performed using SAS® version 9.4 or later.

## 10. Tables, Figures, and Listings

### 10.1 Tables

- Serious adverse events
- Laboratory abnormalities

### 10.2 Figures
- Patient disposition flowchart
- Kaplan-Meier plot for time to recurrence (if sufficient data available)

### 10.3 Listings
- Patient demographics
- Adverse events
- Concomitant medications
- Protocol deviations

## 11. Study Limitations

Due to early study termination:
- Limited follow-up time (median 10.3 months)
- Insufficient power for planned efficacy comparisons
- Descriptive analysis approach only
- Incomplete data collection for planned endpoints

**Note:** This SAP reflects the actual conduct and early termination of the study. The primary analysis is descriptive in nature due to the sponsor's decision to terminate the study prematurely on August 24, 2016.
