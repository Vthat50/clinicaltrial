**Study Title:** GSK2302032A Antigen-Specific Cancer Immunotherapeutic as Adjuvant Therapy in Patients With Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

**NCT ID:** NCT01853878  
**Sponsor:** GlaxoSmithKline  
**Phase:** Phase 2  
**SAP Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** [TO BE CONFIRMED]

## 1. STUDY OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Study Design
- Design: Randomized, parallel-group, quadruple-masked, placebo-controlled study
- Target Enrollment: 137 patients
- Study Population: Patients with radically resected NSCLC (pathological stage IA-T1b, IB, II or IIIA) with PRAME expression

### 1.2 Treatment Arms
- **Experimental Group:** GSK2302032A (Recombinant PRAME protein combined with the AS15 Adjuvant System)
- **Control Group:** Placebo

### 1.3 Study Termination
The study was terminated early on August 24, 2016. After unblinding, subjects in the active treatment group who were willing continued treatment, while placebo group subjects were withdrawn. Active follow-up was discontinued after treatment completion.

## 2. STUDY OBJECTIVES AND ENDPOINTS

### 2.1 Primary Objective
To evaluate the time to occurrence of any recurrence of disease in patients with resected NSCLC treated with GSK2302032A versus placebo.

### 2.2 Primary Endpoint
**Time to Occurrence of Any Recurrence of Disease**
- Measurement Period: From Week 1 to Week 112
- Analysis Note: Due to early study termination, not all data were available for full analysis. Median follow-up time was 10.3 months.

## 3. STUDY POPULATIONS

### 3.1 Analysis Populations
- **Primary Analysis Population:** All randomized patients [TO BE CONFIRMED]
- **Safety Population:** All patients who received at least one dose of study treatment [TO BE CONFIRMED]

### 3.2 Key Inclusion Criteria
- Radically resected NSCLC with pathological stage IA-T1b, IB, II or IIIA
- Tumor shows expression of PRAME
- Age ≥ 18 years at time of first consent
- ECOG performance status of 0, 1, or 2 at randomization
- Free of disease confirmed by post-surgery imaging
- Adequate bone-marrow reserve, renal, hepatic and adrenal function

## 4. STATISTICAL METHODS

### 4.1 General Principles
All clinical and safety data collected in the study were analyzed descriptively due to early study termination.

### 4.2 Primary Analysis Method
**Person-Year Rate Calculation:**
- Person-year rate in each group = number of patients reporting at least one recurrence of disease (n) / sum of follow-up period expressed in years (T[year])
- Rates will be calculated separately for each treatment group

### 4.3 Additional Analyses
Given the early termination, primary and secondary objectives were not assessed as originally planned. All analyses will be descriptive in nature.

## 6. EFFICACY ANALYSES

### 6.1 Primary Efficacy Analysis
Due to early study termination, the primary endpoint will be analyzed descriptively:

- Time to recurrence (median, range) for patients who experienced recurrence
- Follow-up time summary statistics

### 6.2 Stratification Factors
The following stratification factors will be considered in descriptive summaries:
- PRAME expression levels
