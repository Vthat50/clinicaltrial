# STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN

**Study Title:** A Phase 1b Trial of Hu5F9-G4 in Combination With Avelumab in Solid Tumor Patients and Checkpoint-Inhibitor-Naive Ovarian Cancer Patients Who Progress Within 6 Months of Prior Platinum Chemotherapy

**NCT ID:** NCT03558139  
**Sponsor:** Gilead Sciences  
**Phase:** Phase 1b  
**Study Design:** Non-randomized, Sequential Assignment, Open-label  

---

## 1. STUDY OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Study Objectives

**Primary Objectives:**
- To investigate the safety and tolerability of magrolimab in combination with avelumab in participants with advanced solid tumors (Part 1)
- To confirm the safety and tolerability of this combination and evaluate the anti-tumor activity in participants with checkpoint inhibitor-naive ovarian cancer who have previously progressed within 1-6 months of receiving platinum chemotherapy (Part 2)

**Secondary Objectives:**
- To determine the Recommended Phase 2 Dose and Schedule (RP2DS) of magrolimab in combination with avelumab
- To evaluate pharmacokinetic parameters of magrolimab
- To assess anti-drug antibody formation to magrolimab
- To evaluate anti-tumor activity in ovarian cancer participants

### 1.2 Study Design
- **Study Type:** Interventional, Phase 1b
- **Allocation:** Non-randomized
- **Intervention Model:** Sequential assignment
- **Masking:** Open-label
- **Target Enrollment:** 34 participants

### 1.3 Treatment Arms

**Part 1 (Safety Run-in):**
- **Arm 1:** Magrolimab 30 mg/kg + Avelumab 800 mg
- **Arm 2:** Magrolimab 45 mg/kg + Avelumab 800 mg

**Part 2 (Ovarian Cancer Expansion):**
- **Arm 3:** Magrolimab 45 mg/kg + Avelumab 800 mg (Ovarian Cancer)

## 2. STUDY ENDPOINTS

### 2.1 Primary Endpoints

1. **Percentage of Participants Experiencing Dose Limiting Toxicities (DLTs) in Safety Run-in (Part 1)**
   - Time Frame: From the first dose date up to 5 weeks
   - Population: Safety Run-in participants (Parts 1)

2. **Percentage of Participants Experiencing Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs)**
   - Time Frame: First dose date up to last dose plus 30 days (maximum treatment duration 18.3 months)
   - Population: All treated participants

3. **Percentage of Participants With Objective Response Rate (ORR) Assessed by RECIST v1.1 in Participants With Ovarian Cancer**
   - Time Frame: From screening until 26.2 months
   - Population: Ovarian cancer participants (Part 2)

### 2.2 Secondary Endpoints

1. **Recommended Phase 2 Dose and Schedule (RP2DS) of Magrolimab in Combination With Avelumab**
   - Time Frame: From the first dose date up to 5 weeks
   - Population: Safety Run-in participants (Part 1)

2. **Serum Concentrations of Magrolimab**
   - Time Frame: Multiple timepoints from Cycle 1 Day 1 through Safety Follow-up
   - Population: Safety Run-in participants (Part 1)

3. **Percentage of Participants With Anti-Drug Antibody to Magrolimab**
   - Time Frame: From Day 1 of Cycle 1 up to Safety Follow-up
   - Population: Safety Run-in participants (Part 1)

4. **Percentage of Participants With Objective Response According to GCIG Criteria in Ovarian Cancer**
   - Time Frame: From Screening until 26.2 months
   - Population: Ovarian cancer participants (Part 2)

5. **Duration of Response (DOR) as Per GCIG Criteria in Participants With Ovarian Cancer**
   - Time Frame: From initial response until disease progression or maximum time on study (26.2 months)
   - Population: Ovarian cancer participants with objective response (Part 2)

6. **Progression-free Survival (PFS) in Participants With Ovarian Cancer**
   - Time Frame: From first dose date to disease progression, death or maximum time on study (26.2 months)
   - Population: Ovarian cancer participants (Part 2)

7. **Overall Survival (OS) in Participants With Ovarian Cancer**
   - Time Frame: From first dose date to death or maximum time on study (26.2 months)
   - Population: Ovarian cancer participants (Part 2)

8. **Percent Change of Immune Cells by Immunohistochemistry in Participants With Ovarian Cancer**
   - Time Frame: Screening and Day 1 Cycle 3
   - Population: Ovarian cancer participants with paired biopsies (Part 2)

## 3. ANALYSIS POPULATIONS

### 3.1 Analysis Sets

**All Treated Population (ATP):** All participants who received at least one dose of any study treatment

**Safety Population:** All participants who received at least one dose of any study treatment (same as ATP)

**DLT Evaluable Population:** All participants in Part 1 who complete the 5-week DLT assessment period or experience a DLT during this period

**Efficacy Population:** All participants who received at least one dose of study treatment and have at least one post-baseline tumor assessment (or early progression)

**Pharmacokinetic Population:** All participants in Part 1 who received at least one dose of magrolimab and have at least one evaluable PK sample

**Immunogenicity Population:** All participants in Part 1 who received at least one dose of magrolimab and have at least one post-baseline anti-drug antibody assessment

### 4.4 Efficacy Analysis

**Objective Response Rate (ORR):**

1. **ORR by RECIST v1.1 (Primary - Ovarian Cancer):**
   - Definition: Percentage of participants with CR + PR
   - Analysis: Exact binomial confidence intervals (95% CI)
   - Population: Efficacy Population (Part 2)

2. **ORR by GCIG Criteria (Secondary - Ovarian Cancer):**
   - Definition: Percentage of participants with CR + PR per GCIG criteria
   - Analysis: Exact binomial confidence intervals (95% CI)
   - Population: Efficacy Population (Part 2)

**Time-to-Event Endpoints:**

3. **Duration of Response (DOR):**
   - Definition: Time from initial response (CR or PR) until disease progression per GCIG criteria
   - Method: Kaplan-Meier estimation
   - Summary Statistics: Median and 95% CI, 6-month and 12-month rates
   - Population: Efficacy Population with objective response (Part 2)
   - Censoring: Participants without progression at last tumor assessment

4. **Progression-free Survival (PFS):**
   - Definition: Time from first dose to disease progression per RECIST v1.1 or death
   - Method: Kaplan-Meier estimation
   - Summary Statistics: Median and 95% CI, 6-month and 12-month rates
   - Population: Efficacy Population (Part 2)
   - Censoring: Participants without progression or death at last tumor assessment

5. **Overall Survival (OS):**
   - Definition: Time from first dose to death from any cause
   - Method: Kaplan-Meier estimation
   - Summary Statistics: Median and 95% CI, 6-month and 12-month rates
   - Population: Efficacy Population (Part 2)
   - Censoring: Participants alive at last known alive date

**Biomarker Analysis:**

6. **Percent Change in CD68+ Macrophages:**

   - Method: [TO BE CONFIRMED - specific statistical test not specified]
   - Population: Ovarian cancer participants with paired biopsies
