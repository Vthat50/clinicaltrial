## 1. STUDY INFORMATION

### 1.1 Study Overview
This is a Phase 1b, non-randomized, sequential assignment, open-label study evaluating magrolimab (Hu5F9-G4) in combination with avelumab in participants with solid tumors and checkpoint-inhibitor-naive ovarian cancer participants who progress within 6 months of prior platinum chemotherapy.

**Study Phase:** Phase 1b  
**Study Design:** Non-randomized, sequential assignment, open-label  
**Total Enrollment:** 34 participants <!-- Source 1: SAMPLE_SIZE -->  
**Study Status:** Completed  
**Study Period:** May 23, 2018 to December 3, 2020

### 1.2 Regulatory Standards
- **Adverse Event Coding:** Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities (MedDRA) Version 26.1
- **Severity Grading:** National Cancer Institute Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (NCI-CTCAE) Version 5.0
- **Concomitant Medication Coding:** World Health Organization Drug Dictionary (WHO-DD) March 2024

## 2. STUDY OBJECTIVES AND ENDPOINTS

### 2.1 Primary Objectives and Endpoints

**Part 1 (Safety Run-in):**
- **Primary Objective:** To determine the recommended Phase 2 dose and schedule (RP2DS) of magrolimab in combination with avelumab <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->
- **Primary Endpoint:** Percentage of Participants Experiencing Dose Limiting Toxicities (DLTs) in Safety Run-in (Part 1) <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->

**Part 2 (Efficacy Evaluation):**
- **Primary Objective:** To evaluate preliminary efficacy in ovarian cancer participants
- **Primary Endpoint:** Percentage of Participants With Objective Response (ORR) Assessed by RECIST Version 1.1 in Participants With Ovarian Cancer <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->

### 2.2 Secondary Endpoints

**Safety Endpoints:**
- Percentage of Participants Experiencing Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs) <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->
- Percentage of Participants With Transient Anti-Drug Antibody to Magrolimab <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->

**Efficacy Endpoints:**
- Percentage of Participants With Objective Response According to GCIG Criteria in Ovarian Cancer <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->
- Duration of Response (DOR) as Per GCIG Criteria <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->
- Progression-free Survival (PFS) <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->
- Overall Survival (OS) <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->

**Pharmacokinetic Endpoints:**
- Serum Concentrations of Magrolimab - Safety Run-in <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->

**Biomarker Endpoints:**
- Percent Change of Immune Cells by Immunohistochemistry <!-- Source 1: ENDPOINT -->

## 3. STUDY DESIGN

### 3.1 Study Structure
The study consists of two parts:

**Part 1: Safety Run-in Cohort**
- Population: Participants with pathologically confirmed advanced solid tumors <!-- Source 1: POPULATION -->
- Design: 3+3 dose escalation design <!-- Source 2: power calculations -->
- Dose levels: 30 mg/kg and 45 mg/kg magrolimab + 800 mg avelumab

**Part 2: Ovarian Cancer Expansion Cohort**
- Population: Histologically or cytologically confirmed, epithelial ovarian cancer participants <!-- Source 1: POPULATION -->
- Dose: RP2DS determined from Part 1

### 3.2 Treatment Schema
- **Cycle 1:** 35 days
- **Cycle 2 and subsequent cycles:** 28 days
- **Priming dose:** 1 mg/kg magrolimab on Day 1 of Cycle 1
- **Maintenance dose:** 30 mg/kg or 45 mg/kg magrolimab per dose level
- **Avelumab:** 800 mg fixed dose

### 3.3 DLT Assessment
- **Assessment Period:** 5 weeks (Cycle 1) <!-- Source 1: METHOD -->
- **DLT Threshold:** <33% DLT rate for RP2DS determination <!-- Source 1: METHOD -->

## 5. STATISTICAL METHODS

### 5.1 General Principles
- **Statistical Software:** [INFERRED: SAS Version 9.4 or later]
- **Significance Level:** α = 0.05 <!-- Source 2: power calculations -->
- **Confidence Intervals:** 95% confidence intervals will be provided where appropriate
- **Missing Data:** No imputation for missing data unless otherwise specified

### 5.2 Primary Endpoint Analyses

**Part 1: DLT Rate Analysis**
The percentage of participants experiencing DLTs will be summarized by dose level with exact 95% confidence intervals using the Clopper-Pearson method. The RP2DS will be the highest dose level with a DLT rate <33% <!-- Source 1: METHOD -->.

**Part 2: Objective Response Rate (RECIST 1.1)**
The ORR will be calculated as the proportion of participants with complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) assessed by RECIST Version 1.1 <!-- Source 1: METHOD -->. The ORR will be presented with exact 95% confidence intervals using the Clopper-Pearson method.

### 5.3 Secondary Endpoint Analyses

**Time-to-Event Endpoints**
For PFS, OS, and DOR, the following analyses will be performed:
- Kaplan-Meier estimates <!-- Source 1: METHOD --> with 95% confidence intervals
- Median time-to-event with 95% confidence intervals
- Kaplan-Meier curves will be presented

**GCIG Response Analysis**
Objective response according to GCIG criteria <!-- Source 1: METHOD --> will be analyzed similar to RECIST 1.1 response, with ORR and exact 95% confidence intervals.

**Biomarker Analysis**
Percent change of immune cells by CD68 immunohistochemistry staining <!-- Source 1: METHOD --> will be analyzed using paired tumor biopsies. Descriptive statistics and changes from baseline will be summarized.

### 5.4 Pharmacokinetic Analysis [INFERRED]
Serum concentrations of magrolimab will be summarized descriptively by dose level and time point. Non-compartmental analysis methods may be used to derive PK parameters.

## 6. SAMPLE SIZE AND POWER

### 6.1 Sample Size Determination
**Calculation Method:** 3+3 design <!-- Source 2: power calculations -->  
**Part 1 Sample Size:** 18 participants <!-- Source 2: power calculations -->  
**Total Enrollment:** 34 participants <!-- Source 1: SAMPLE_SIZE -->  
**Statistical Assumptions:** α = 0.05, Power = 0.8 <!-- Source 2: power calculations -->

### 6.2 Rationale
The 3+3 design provides appropriate safety evaluation for dose escalation in Part 1. The total sample size of 34 participants allows for adequate safety run-in evaluation and preliminary efficacy assessment in the ovarian cancer expansion cohort.

Ongoing safety reviews will be conducted by the Safety Review Committee after each dose level in Part 1 to determine dose escalation decisions and RP2DS selection. No formal interim efficacy analyses are planned [INFERRED].

## 8. MISSING DATA HANDLING

### 8.1 General Approach
No imputation will be performed for missing data. Analyses will be based on observed data only, unless otherwise specified.

### 8.2 Time-to-Event Endpoints
For time-to-event analyses, participants without events will be censored at the last known alive date or last disease assessment date, as appropriate.

### 8.3 Response Assessments
Participants with missing or unevaluable response assessments will be considered non-responders for response rate calculations.

## 11. SAFETY ANALYSES

### 11.1 Safety Analysis Principles
All safety analyses will be performed on the Safety Analysis Set. TEAEs will be coded using MedDRA Version 26.1 and graded according to NCI-CTCAE Version 5.0.

### 11.2 Adverse Events Summary
Treatment-emergent adverse events are defined as AEs that occur on or after the first dose of study treatment through 30 days after the last dose.

### 11.3 Standard TEAE Summary Tables

The following 13 TEAE summary tables will be generated:

**Table S.1: Overview of Adverse Events**

- Any TEAE
- Grade ≥3 TEAEs  
- Serious TEAEs
- TEAEs leading to study drug discontinuation
- TEAEs leading to dose modification
- TEAEs leading to death
- Treatment-related TEAEs

**Table S.2: TEAEs by System Organ Class and Preferred Term**
Detailed TEAE listing by MedDRA hierarchy (SOC and PT) showing number and percentage of participants with each TEAE.

**Table S.3: TEAEs by Preferred Term (≥10% incidence)**
Most common TEAEs occurring in ≥10% of participants, sorted by decreasing frequency.

**Table S.4: Treatment-Related Adverse Events by SOC and PT**
TEAEs assessed by investigator as related to magrolimab or avelumab, organized by MedDRA SOC and PT.

**Table S.5: Treatment-Related AEs by PT (≥5% incidence)**
Most common treatment-related TEAEs occurring in ≥5% of participants.

**Table S.6: Grade 3 or Higher TEAEs by SOC and PT**
Severe and life-threatening TEAEs (CTCAE Grade ≥3) by MedDRA SOC and PT.

**Table S.7: Grade 3 or Higher Treatment-Related AEs by SOC and PT**
Grade ≥3 TEAEs assessed as treatment-related by MedDRA SOC and PT.

**Table S.10: TEAEs Leading to Dose Modification**

**Table S.11: TEAEs Leading to Death**
All deaths with primary cause and relationship to study treatment.

**Table S.12: Dose Limiting Toxicities**
DLTs occurring during the 5-week DLT assessment period by dose level.

**Table S.13: Immune-Related Adverse Events** [INFERRED]
AEs of special interest for immunotherapy combinations (irAEs, infusion reactions).

### 11.4 Laboratory Safety Analyses [INFERRED]
Laboratory parameters will be summarized descriptively and shifts from baseline will be analyzed. Notable laboratory abnormalities will be flagged according to predefined criteria.
