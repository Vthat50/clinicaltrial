A. Individual Clinical Study Information 
 
1. Title of study: 
 
The PROTECT Study: Peginterferon lambda-1a for the prevention and treatment of SARS-CoV-2 (COVID-19) 
infection. 
2. Purpose of study: 
 
The PROTECT study was designed to test the hypothesis that lambda interferon would be safe and effective 
for the prevention and early treatment of SARS-CoV-2 infection in persons with high-risk exposure to COVID-
19 (e.g., persons with household contacts).    
 
3. Research subject population: 
 
All participants ≥ 18 years of age who are household contacts of individuals with confirmed COVID-19 infection 
(within 7 days of diagnosis) without self-report of symptoms of acute respiratory illness (fever, cough or 
shortness of breath).    
 
4. Total number of subjects initially planned for inclusion in the study: 
 
Up to 164 participants will be enrolled in the study for a sample of 110 participants at risk for SARS-CoV-2 
infection due to household exposure to an individual with COVID-19 (Prevention Cohort). The treatment 
cohort sample will be determined by the proportion of asymptomatic persons with SARS-CoV-2 detected at 
study entry constituting an exploratory cohort.   
 
5. Total number of subjects entered into the study to date: 
 
6 subjects enrolled. Refer to Appendix A. 
   
6. Number of subjects whose participation in the study was completed as planned: 
 
6 subjects. 
 
7. Number of subjects who dropped out of the study early for any reason: 
 
None. 
 
8. Brief description of final study results:  
 
We enrolled 6 participants; all were SARS-CoV-2 NAT negative at study entry and entered the prevention study 
cohort.  Of these, two participants were randomized to lambda interferon injections and four participants 
were randomized to placebo.  Of the placebo participants, one was infected with SARS-CoV-2, detected by PCR 
on nasopharyngeal samples on day 7; the participant had evidence of COVID-19 seroconversion on day 28.  
The participant did not require hospitalization for COVID-19 and recovered without sequelae.   
 
No participants were SARS-CoV-2 NAT positive at entry and none entered the treatment cohort.  Accordingly, 
we were not able to assess the drug in early SARS CoV-2 infection.    
 
B. Summary Investigational Drug Information  
 
1. Summary of the most frequent and most serious adverse experiences by body system:  
 
In this limited sample size, we did not detect clinical adverse events due to lambda interferon and observed 
mild AST elevations (ALT normal; AST 43 U/L) in one of the lambda interferon treated participants which were 
resolved by end of the study period.  No other significant adverse events were reported.  
 
Refer to Appendix B.  
 
2. Summary of all IND Safety Reports submitted during the previous year: 
 
None. 
 
3. List of research subjects who died during participation in clinical studies of the investigational drug 
(i.e., inclusive of all clinical studies conducted under the IND) 
 
No research subjects died while participating in clinical studies under this IND.    
 
4. List of research subjects whose participation in clinical studies of the investigational drug was 
terminated in association with an adverse experience (i.e., inclusive of all clinical studies conducted 
under the IND):  
 
No research subjects were terminated in association with an adverse experience) from participation in a 
clinical study under this IND. 
 
5. Description of new information pertinent to understanding the actions of the investigational drug: 
 
The role of peginterferon lambda-1a for the treatment and prevention of SARS-CoV-2 infection remains under 
investigation.  The hypothesis that peginterferon lambda-1a may prevent infection with SARS-CoV-2 may 
warrant further investigation.  However, we were not able to test this hypothesis in this single center study 
due to challenges with participant enrollment, particularly in the era of COVID-19 vaccine and prevention with 
monoclonal antibodies.    
 
6. List of preclinical studies (including in-vitro and animal studies) completed or in progress during the 
past year, and a summary of major preclinical findings: 
 
No preclinical studies completed or in progress during the past year. 
 
7. Summary of any significant manufacturing or microbiological changes made during the past year: 
 
No significant manufacturing or microbiological changes were made during the past year.
