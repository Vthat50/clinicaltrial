STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: A Phase II, Randomized, Open, Controlled Study to Evaluate the Safety and Immunogenicity of Different Formulations of a Pandemic Influenza Vaccine Candidate (Split Virus Formulation Adjuvanted With AS03) Given Following a Two-administration Schedule (21 Days Apart) in Children Between 3 and 9 Years of Age.
NCT ID: NCT00502593
Phase: PHASE2
Enrollment: 138
Endpoint Type: OS
