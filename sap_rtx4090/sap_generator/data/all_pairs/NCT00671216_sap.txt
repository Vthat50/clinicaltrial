STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: A Single Centre, Randomised, Placebo-controlled, Four-way Cross Over Study to Assess the Safety, Tolerability, Pharmacodynamics and Pharmacokinetics of Single Inhaled Doses of GSK233705 and GW642444 as Monotherapies and in Combination in Healthy Subjects.
NCT ID: NCT00671216
Phase: PHASE1
Enrollment: 16
Endpoint Type: SAFETY

PRIMARY ANALYSIS
  Outcome: General safety endpoints: measured over 24 hours post dose for all 4 dose periods, heart rate, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, 12- lead ECG and lung function and clinical laboratory safety tests. Adverse events over whole study.
  Time Frame: N/A
