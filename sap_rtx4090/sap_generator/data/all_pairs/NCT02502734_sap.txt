STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: A Randomised, Double-blind, Two-way Crossover Study to Investigate the Effect of Inhaled Fluticasone Furoate on Short-term Growth in Paediatric Subjects With Asthma
NCT ID: NCT02502734
Phase: PHASE3
Enrollment: 60
Endpoint Type: SAFETY

PRIMARY ANALYSIS
  Outcome: Mean Growth Rate in Lower-leg Growth, as Determined by Knemometry.
  Time Frame: N/A
  Description: Lower leg growth rate was assessed in growth population as change in the lower leg length from start to end of each 2-week period, divided by time interval (number of days) between the two measurements, multiplied by 7.
