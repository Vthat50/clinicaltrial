STUDY DESIGN
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Phase: PHASE1
Allocation: RANDOMIZED
Intervention Model: CROSSOVER
Primary Purpose: TREATMENT
Masking: DOUBLE
Enrollment: 16

TREATMENT ARMS
  Arm 1: Period 1
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description: Subjects will receive first placebo, then GSK233705, GW642444 and combination of GSK233705 and GW642444
  Arm 2: Period 2
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description: Subjects will receive first combination of GSK233705 and GW642444, then placebo, GSK233705 and GW642444
  Arm 3: Period 3
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description: Subjects will receive first GSK233705, then GW642444, combination of GSK233705 and GW642444 and later placebo
  Arm 4: Period 4
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description: Subjects will receive first GW642444, then combination of GSK233705 and GW642444, placebo, and later GSK233705

INTERVENTIONS
  1. GSK233705 (DRUG)
     Description: Subjects will receive 200 (mcg) microgram once daily as a single dose
  2. GW642444 (DRUG)
     Description: Subjects will receive 50 mcg once daily as a single dose
  3. GSK233705 and GW642444 (DRUG)
     Description: Subjects will receive combination of GSK233705 200 mcg and GW642444 50 mcg as a single oral dose
  4. Placebo (DRUG)
     Description: Placebo matching study medication will be inhaled by subjects

  1. General safety endpoints: measured over 24 hours post dose for all 4 dose periods, heart rate, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, 12- lead ECG and lung function and clinical laboratory safety tests. Adverse events over whole study.
     Time Frame: Up to Day 2

SECONDARY OUTCOMES
  1. Blood levels of GW642444, Blood levels of GSK233705, Blood Levels of potassium
     Time Frame: Pre-dose; Day 1- 5, 15 and 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4, 5,6, 8, 12, 16 and 24 hours and Day 2

* Healthy as determined by a responsible physician, based on a medical evaluation including medical history, physical examination, laboratory tests and cardiac monitoring.
* Male or female between 18 and 65 years of age.

STUDY DESCRIPTION
Brief Summary:
GW642444 and GSK233705 are in development for treatment of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease. Development of these two inhaled drugs as a combination therapy would have potential for improved patient benefit as they both work through different mechanisms and the combined bronchodilatory effect might be additive. This study will look at the this combination, for the first time, in healthy subjects.
