STUDY: Impact of Resveratrol on Brain Function and Structure
NCT ID: NCT02621554
Sponsor: Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences

Phase: NA
Design: PARALLEL
Masking: TRIPLE
Enrollment: 60

Arm: resveratrol supplementation (EXPERIMENTAL)
Arm: placebo supplementation (PLACEBO_COMPARATOR)
Intervention: resveratrol supplementation
Intervention: Placebo

PRIMARY: Change from baseline Verbal Learning Task Scores at 6 months
  Time: Baseline (timepoint 0), after 6 months (timepoint 6 months)

ELIGIBILITY:
Inclusion Criteria:

* subjects who are healthy or with subjective memory complaints
* older than 60 years old
* normal to overweight participants (BMI: 22-40 kg/sqm)

Exclusion Criteria:

* dementia
* Psychiatric diseases
* severe untreated internal diseases
* diabetes
* younger than 60 years
* BMI \< 22 or \>40

SUMMARY:
The study will investigate whether resveratrol could provide positive effects on memory and brain structures and functions in healthy elderly participants.
