STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Gabapentin as Preanesthetic Medication to Fast Procedures in Pediatric´s Oncology
Phase: PHASE4
Endpoint: OTHER
PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: The intensity of anxiety in children by Modified Yale Preoperative Anxiety Scale (mYPAS) before an oncologic procedure under Gabapentin or placebo effects.
Frame: 
Method: Children, ranging from 1 to 6 years old, will receive drugs (placebo, gabapentin 15mg/kg or 30mg/kg) 1 to 2 hours before the oncologic procedure (myelogram and/ or puncture lumbar). They will be submitted to the same general anesthesia protocol (sevoflurane 8% plus N2O and oxygen) and postoperative analgesia.
