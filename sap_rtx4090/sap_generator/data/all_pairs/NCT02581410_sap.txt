STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Immunogenicity and Safety Study of GSK Biologicals' Herpes Zoster Vaccine GSK1437173A in Adults ≥ 65 Years of Age With and Without Zostavax® Vaccination at Least 5 Years Earlier
NCT ID: NCT02581410
Phase: PHASE3
Enrollment: 430
Endpoint Type: OS

PRIMARY ANALYSIS
  Outcome: Number of Subjects With Solicited General Symptoms
  Time Frame: N/A
  Description: Assessed solicited general symptoms were fatigue, gastrointestinal symptoms (included nausea, vomiting, diarrhea and/or abdominal pain), headache, myalgia, shivering and fever \[defined as axillary temperature equal to or above 37.5 degrees Celsius (°C)\] . Any = Occurrence of the symptom regardless of its intensity grade. Grade 3 symptom = symptom that prevented normal activity. Grade 3 fever = fever \> 39.0 °C. Related = symptom assessed by the investigator as related to the vaccination.
  Outcome: Anti-glycoprotein E (Anti-gE) Antibody (Ab) Concentrations
  Time Frame: N/A
  Description: Varicella Zoster Virus (VZV) gE Ab.Immunoglobulin G (IgG) was determined by Enzyme Linked Immunosorbent Assay (ELISA). Concentrations are presented as geometric mean concentrations (GMCs), expressed in milliinternational units per millilitre (mIU/mL). Geometric mean antibody concentrations were adjusted for group-matching variable.
  Outcome: Number of Subjects With Solicited Local Symptoms
  Time Frame: N/A
  Description: Assessed solicited local symptoms were pain, redness and swelling. Any = Occurrence of the symptom regardless of its intensity grade. Grade 3 pain = Significant pain at rest that prevented normal everyday activities. Grade 3 redness/swelling = redness/swelling spreading beyond 100 millimeters (mm) of injection site.
  Outcome: Number of Days With Solicited Local Symptoms
  Time Frame: N/A
  Description: Solicited local symptoms were assessed during the 7-day (Days 0-6) period after each dose.
  Outcome: Number of Days With Solicited General Symptoms
  Time Frame: N/A
  Description: Solicited general symptoms were assessed during the 7-day (Days 0-6) period after each dose.

  Outcome: Number of Subjects With Any and Related Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)
  Time Frame: N/A

  Outcome: Number of Subjects With Any Potential Immune-mediated Diseases (pIMDs)
