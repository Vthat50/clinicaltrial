STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Individualizing Dose of Growth Hormone to Maintain Normal Growth Velocity After Fulfilled Catch up Growth in Children
Phase: PHASE2/PHASE3
Endpoint: OTHER
PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: The Proportion of Children Maintaining Normal Growth Velocity
Frame: 
Method: The proportion of children in the intervention 1 group (=reduced dose) that maintained an individual ΔheightSDS within ±0.3 during the first year of the Maintenance trial, compared to the proportion of children in the intervention 2 group (non-reduced dose)
