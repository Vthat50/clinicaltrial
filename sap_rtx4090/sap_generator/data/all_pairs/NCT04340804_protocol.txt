STUDY: Socioeconomic Position and the Effect of kcal Labelling and PACE Labelling on Self-served Portion Size
NCT ID: NCT04340804
Sponsor: University of Liverpool

Phase: NA
Design: PARALLEL
Masking: NONE
Enrollment: 1800

Arm: Kcal labelling (EXPERIMENTAL)
Arm: PACE labelling (EXPERIMENTAL)
Arm: Kcal and PACE labelling (EXPERIMENTAL)
Arm: No labelling (NO_INTERVENTION)
Intervention: Kcal labelling
Intervention: PACE labelling

PRIMARY: Total self-served energy (kcal)
  Time: 10 minutes (the time frame denotes time taken to perform the portion selection task and there is no follow up)
  Desc: Energy content of the portion selected for each dish.

ELIGIBILITY:
Inclusion Criteria:

* United Kingdom residents
* Age ≥ 18
* Fluent in English
* Have access to a computer and Internet

Exclusion Criteria:

* Have any dietary restriction (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, sugar-free, diary or lactose free, food allergy)

SUMMARY:
Participants will take part in an online survey. They will be asked to choose the amount of food they would like to eat based on pictures of 18 dishes sequentially displayed on the screen. They will be randomly allocated to four different groups: kcal labelling, PACE labelling (Physical Activity Calorie Equivalent: minutes to walk to burn off the calories), kcal and PACE labelling combined, no labelling, in a between subject design. The main outcome variable is the total "self-served" energy for each dish (in kcal).
