STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Long-Term Follow-Up Studies at Year 6, 7, 8, 9, 10: 2 Formulations of Combined Hepatitis A/B Vaccine Compared in Subjects Aged 12-15 Years
NCT ID: NCT00197119
Phase: PHASE3
Endpoint Type: OS

PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Outcome: Number of Subjects With Anti-hepatitis A Virus (HAV) Antibody Concentrations Above the Cut-off Value
Time Frame: N/A
Analysis: Anti-HAV antibody concentration cut-off value assessed was ≥ 15 milli-International Units per milliliter (mIU/mL).
Outcome: Number of Subjects With Anti-Hepatitis B Surface Antigen (HBs) Antibody Concentrations Above the Cut-Off Value
Time Frame: N/A
Analysis: Anti-HBs antibody concentration cut-off value assessed was ≥ 3.3 mIU/mL.
Outcome: Serious Adverse Events (SAE) Causally Related to Primary Vaccination or Related to Hepatitis A or B Infection or Related to Study Participation (Blood Sampling)
Time Frame: N/A
Analysis: An SAE is any untoward medical occurrence that:

results in death, is life-threatening, requires hospitalization or prolongation of existing hospitalization, results in disability/ incapacity, is a congenital anomaly/birth defect in the offspring of a study subject, or may evolve into one of the outcomes listed above.
