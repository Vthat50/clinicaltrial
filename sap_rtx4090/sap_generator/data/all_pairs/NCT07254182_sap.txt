STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Hand Cooling With Ice Packs in Healthy Young Men
Phase: NA
Endpoint: OTHER
PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Minimum dorsal-hand skin temperature during rewarming (IV-X), by infrared thermography
Frame: 
Method: Region-of-interest (ROI) mean skin temperature (Tsk; °C) on the dorsal hand measured with infrared thermography (IRT; FLIR SC640). The primary estimand is the between-group mean difference (Ice - Thermoneutral) in the nadir (minimum) across rewarming time points IV-X, with 95% confidence intervals (CI). Lower values indicate greater cooling.
