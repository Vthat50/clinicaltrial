STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Dose-escalation Study of Oral Administration of S 055746 in Patients With Acute Myeloid Leukaemia or Myelodysplastic Syndrome
Phase: PHASE1
Endpoint: SAFETY
PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Incidence of Adverse Events (AEs)
Frame: 
Method: Characterized by severity and seriousness of AEs, laboratory abnormalities and other safety parameters such as electrocardiogram (ECG) changes
Measure: Maximum Tolerated Dose (MTD)
Frame: 
Method: MTD is the highest drug dosage that is unlikely (\<25% posterior probability) to cause DLT in more than 33% of the treated patients in the first cycle of S 055746 treatment.
