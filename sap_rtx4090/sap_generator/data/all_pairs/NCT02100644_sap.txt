STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Valproate Dose Reduction and Its Clinical Evaluation by Introducing Lamotrigine in Japanese Women With Epilepsy - Single Arm, Multicenter, and Open-label Study
Phase: PHASE4
Endpoint: OTHER
PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Percentage of Participants Who Achieved Reduction in Daily VPA Dose
Frame: 
Method: The VPA dose reduction from Baseline is defined as post VPA dose minus the Baseline VPA dose \< 0. Baseline VPA dose is the dose at the Baseline visit (Week 0) and the post VPA dose is the last VPA dose during the LTG and VPA Maintenance Phase. Percentage of participants with dose reduction during the LTG and VPA Maintenance Phase is presented.
Measure: Percent Change in the VPA Dose
Frame: 
Method: Percent change in VPA dose is calculated as (pre-dose - post-dose) / pre-dose x 100. Pre-dose is the VPA dose at the Baseline visit and post-dose is the last VPA dose during the LTG and VPA Maintenance Phase.
