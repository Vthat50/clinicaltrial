STUDY: Individualizing the Dose of Growth Hormone (GH) to Maintain Normal Growth Velocity After Fulfilled Catch up Growth in Children Within TR 98-0198-003
NCT ID: NCT02879747
Sponsor: Göteborg University

Phase: PHASE2, PHASE3
Design: PARALLEL
Masking: NONE
Enrollment: 99

Arm: Interventional (ACTIVE_COMPARATOR)
Arm: Interventional 2 (ACTIVE_COMPARATOR)
Intervention: Genotropin

PRIMARY: The Proportion of Children Maintaining Normal Growth Velocity
  Time: twelve months
  Desc: The proportion of children in the intervention 1 group (=reduced dose) that maintained an individual ΔheightSDS within ±0.3 during the first year of the Maintenance trial, compared to the proportion of children in the intervention 2 group (non-reduced dose)

ELIGIBILITY:
Inclusion Criteria:

* Participated in the 'GH-dose catch-up study' 98- 0198-003.
* Midparental height reached (difference less than 0.6 SDS)
* Prepubertal at start of the study (girls =B 1, boys: testes :\<; 3ml).
* Signed written informed consent from the patient's parents (and the child if old enough)

Exclusion Criteria:

* Disease affecting growth other than correctly treated hypothyroidism.
* Incapable of following the study protocol (i.e. bad compliance in the previous study).
* Puberty (\> breast stage 2, or testes \> 4ml).
* Poor compliance.

SUMMARY:
The objective was to study whether normal growth velocity can be maintained with adapted GH dosage in GH treated prepubertal children who have responded to GH treatment with fulfilled catch up growth (=difference to target height reached, less than - 0.6 SDS).
