STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: A Randomised Effectiveness Study Comparing Fluticasone Furoate (FF, GW685698)/Vilanterol (VI, GW642444) With Standard Treatment in Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)
Phase: PHASE3
Endpoint: OTHER
PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Mean Annual Rate of Moderate or Severe COPD Exacerbations
Frame: 
Method: Mean annual rate of moderate or severe COPD exacerbations during treatment were assessed. Moderate exacerbation: participant received exacerbation-related prescription of oral corticosteroids and/ or antibiotic (with/without National Health Service \[NHS\] contact) not requiring hospitalisation. Severe exacerbation: an exacerbation-related hospitalisation.
