STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: A First Time in Human Study Exploring Safety, Tolerability, Pharmacokinetics (PK) and Pharmacodynamics (PD) of GSK2618960 in Healthy Volunteers and Patients With Relapsing Remitting Multiple Sclerosis (RRMS)
NCT ID: NCT01808482
Phase: PHASE1
Endpoint Type: SAFETY

PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Outcome: Safety and tolerability of GSK2618960 as assessed by changes in: vital signs, ECG monitoring, haematology, clinical chemistry, urinalysis, and monitoring of AEs in healthy volunteers and MS patients
Time Frame: N/A
Analysis: Safety and tolerability parameters will include recording of vital signs, electrocardiogram (ECG)s, safety labs, and adverse event (AE)s, in Part A/B/C of the study in healthy volunteers and Multiple Sclerosis (MS) patients.
Outcome: Safety and tolerability of GSK2618960 as assessed by changes in MS relapses: Number, duration, severity in MS patients
Time Frame: N/A
Analysis: Safety and tolerability parameters will include recording of the number/duration/severity of MS relapses in Part C of the study in MS patients
