STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: A Randomized, Multi-Center, Blinded, Placebo-Controlled Study Of Mapatumumab ([HGS1012], A Fully Monoclonal Antibody To TRAIL-R1) In Combination With Sorafenib As A First-Line Therapy In Subjects With Advanced Hepatocellular Carcinoma
NCT ID: NCT01258608
Phase: PHASE1/PHASE2
Enrollment: 101
Endpoint Type: PFS

PRIMARY ANALYSIS
  Outcome: Time to Progression-Blinded Independent Central Review (BICR) Assessment
  Time Frame: N/A
  Description: Time to progression is defined as the time from randomization to radiologic disease progression based on blinded independent review (BICR) of imaging scans using modified Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors assessment (mRECIST) for hepatocellular carcinoma. The primary analysis was performed using Kaplan Meier methods. The median time to progression is reported with one-sided 90% confidence interval.
