PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Outcome: Longitudinal Assessment of Participants With Modified Rankin Scale (mRS) <=3
Time Frame: N/A
Analysis: Comparing longitudinal modified Rankin Scale (mRS) scores 0-3 at Day 30 and Day 180. The mRS is a commonly used scale for measuring the degree of disability or dependence in the daily activities of people who have suffered a stroke or other causes of neurological disability. It is scored from 0 (perfect health without symptoms) to 6 (death).
