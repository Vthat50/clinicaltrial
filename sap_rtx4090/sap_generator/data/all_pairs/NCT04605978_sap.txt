STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Efficacy and Safety of S95011 in Primary Sjögren's Syndrome Patients
Phase: PHASE2
Endpoint: OTHER
PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Change in ESSDAI Total Score
Frame: 
Method: Efficacy criterion Eular Sjögren Syndrome Disease Activity index (ESSDAI) is a physician-administered clinical index which has been validated to objectively assess systemic manifestations in Primary Sjögren's Syndrome patients. Scores range from 0 - 123, with a lower score representing less disease activity.
