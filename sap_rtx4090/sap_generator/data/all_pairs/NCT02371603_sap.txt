STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Drug-drug Interaction Study of GSK1278863 With Pioglitazone, Rosuvastatin and Trimethoprim in Healthy Adult Volunteers
Phase: PHASE1
Endpoint: OTHER
PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Composite of PK parameters for GSK1278863 and its 6 predominant metabolites following oral administration of GSK1278863 alone and in combination with steady-state trimethoprim evaluated by measurement of plasma Cmax and AUC (0-infinity) for Part B
Frame: 
Method: The effect of trimethoprim on the pharmacokinetics of GSK1278863 and six predominant metabolites (GSK2391220 \[M2\], GSK2506104 \[M3\], GSK2487818 \[M4\], GSK2506102 \[M5\], GSK2531398 \[M6\], GSK2531401 \[M13\]) will be assessed using the following PK parameters: Cmax and AUC (0-infinity)
Measure: Composite of pharmacokinetic (PK) parameters for pioglitazone following oral administration of pioglitazone alone and in combination with GSK1278863, evaluated by measurement of plasma Cmax and AUC (0-infinity) for Part A
Frame: 
Method: The effect of an oral dose of GSK1278863 on the pharmacokinetics of pioglitazone will be assessed using the following PK parameters: maximum observed concentration (Cmax), area under the concentration-time curve from time zero (pre-dose) extrapolated to infinite time (AUC (0-infinity)
Measure: Composite of PK parameters for rosuvastatin following oral administration of rosuvastatin alone and in combination with GSK1278863 evaluated by measurement of plasma Cmax and AUC (0-infinity) for Part A
Frame: 
Method: The effect of an oral dose of GSK1278863 on the pharmacokinetics of rosuvastatin will be assessed using the following PK parameters: Cmax and AUC (0-infinity)
