STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN
Study: Supporting Parents & Kids Through Lockdown Experiences (SPARKLE).
Phase: NA
Endpoint: OTHER
PRIMARY ANALYSIS
Measure: Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire (SDQ; Goodman, 1997) Conduct Problems
Frame: 
Method: Parent-rated conduct subscale of the SDQ; a five item subscale measuring oppositional, defiant and disruptive behaviour rated on a 3-point Likert scale (not true, somewhat true, and certainly true), with a mix of positive and negatively phrased items. Individual items' scores are summed to derive an overall symptoms subscale score. This measure is also collected at baseline (T1), prior to randomisation.
