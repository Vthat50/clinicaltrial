3 STUDY OVERVIEW  
3.1 Study Design  
This Phase 2, open -label, 2 -part, multicenter study will evaluate the safety, efficacy, PK, and 
pharmacodynamics of eFT508 in combination with avelumab in subjects with microsatellite 
stable relapsed or refractory colorectal cancer.  
The primary objective of Part 1 is to evalu ate the safety and tolerability of escalating doses of 
eFT508 in combination with a fixed dose of avelumab to determine the MTD of eFT508 and 
to select a recommended dose of eFT508 in combination with avelumab for Part 2. The 
primary objective of Part 2 is  to evaluate the efficacy of eFT508 at the recommended dose in 
combination with avelumab or eFT508 monotherapy. Parts 1 and 2 will also evaluate PK and 
pharmacodynamics.

Study therapy will be administered in 4 -week treatment cycles. eFT508 will be taken o rally 
(PO) bid. Avelumab 10 mg/kg will be administered IV on Day 1 and q2wk thereafter. Study 
therapy will be administered until documented disease progression by iRECIST, 
unacceptable toxicity, the subject withdraws consent, the Investigator or Sponsor di scontinue 
the subject, or the study is terminated. Subjects who discontinue study therapy permanently 
will remain on study for the acquisition of safety information for up to 30 days after the last 
dose of study therapy. Thereafter, subjects will be follow ed at 3 - to 6-month intervals, at the 
Sponsor’s discretion, for up to 3 years to collect information on the initiation of first 
subsequent anticancer therapy and survival.  
Safety assessments will include  adverse event  (AE) recording, physical examinations , vital 
sign measurements, ECGs, Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance 
status, and clinical safety laboratory tests (hematology, serum chemistry, thyroid function, 
coagulation, and urinalysis).  
Efficacy assessments will include radiographi c examinations (and physical examinations if 
needed) every 8 weeks. Radiology examinations include CT (and may include positron 
emission tomography [PET], if relevant) or magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) imaging of 
all sites of measurable disease. For subj ects with bone metastases, a bone scan (MRI allowed 
as alternative) should be performed every 16 weeks. Disease response will be evaluated using 
RECIST 1.1 and iRECIST.  
This study will also analyze eFT508 and avelumab PK and pharmacodynamics. 
Pharmacodyna mic analyses will explore target engagement of eFT508, the mechanism of 
action of eFT508, and predictive markers for sensitivity and resistance to eFT508.  
 Note: All subjects will undergo a fresh pre -treatment tumor biopsy and a post -treatment 
biopsy for p harmacodynamic analysis. Genetic analysis may also be performed on blood and 
biopsy tissue samples. Genetic testing is optional. Genetic analysis will include T -cell 
clonality and the analysis of relevant oncogenic mutations including, but not limited to, 
KRAS and p53.  
3.1.1 Dose Escalation in Part 1  
In Part 1, a Dose Limiting Toxicity (DLT) is defined as an AE  meeting specific criteria and 
not primarily related to the underlying malignancy, a comorbid condition, or an intercurrent 
illness. The full list of crite ria for determination of DLTs is given in Protocol Section 3.2.3 . 
3.1.1.1  Dose -Escalation Guidelines  
A standard 3+3 dose -escalation design will be used in Part 1. Dose escalation will be guided 
by the safety data from the first treatment cycle (Day 1 through Day 2 8) in each cohort.  
In each cohort, 3 subjects will be treated with eFT508 and avelumab.

3.1.1.2  Maximum Tolerable Dose  
In Part 1, the maximum tolerable dose (MTD) will be the hi ghest eFT508 dose level 
evaluated at which 1 or fewer of 6 subjects experienced a DLT in Cycle 1.  
The recommended dose for Part 2 may be the MTD or may be a lower dose. Selection of the 
recommended dose will be based on consideration of short - and long -term safety information 
together with the available PK, pharmacodynamics, and efficacy data.  
3.2 Study Centers  
This study will be conducted at 6 to 10 study sites in the United States.

In Part 2, eligible subjects will be randomized  in a ratio of approximately 2:1 to receive 
either eFT508 in combination with avelumab or eFT508 monotherapy. Randomization will 
be implemented centrally through an interactive voice response/inte ractive web response . 
Subjects will be directly assigned a t reatment arm as this is an open -label study .

3.4 Sample Size Determination  
In Part 1, up to approximately 30 DLT -evaluable subjects wi ll be enrolled. The total number 
of subjects will depend upon the number of dose cohorts evaluated and the number of 
subjects treated in each cohort.  
 In Part 2, up to approximately 40 efficacy evaluable subjects will be enrolled and 
randomized in approxim ately a 2:1 ratio to receive eFT508 in combination with avelumab 
(27 subjects) or eFT508 monotherapy (13 subjects). In the combination arm, the Simon 2 -
stage mini -max design to test the null hypothesis that the ORR  is ≤5% against the one -sided 
alternative hypothesis that it is ≥20% will be used.  
Under this design, 13 subjects will be accrued in the first stage. If 0 of 13 subjects have an 
objective tumor response per iRECIST, then randomization to both treatment ar ms will cease 
and the study will stop. If one or more objective tumor responses per iRECIST are seen in 
Stage 1, 14 additional subjects would be accrued in Stage 2 for a total of 27 subjects. The 
null hypothesis will be rejected if ≥4 objective responses a re observed in the total of 27 
subjects (15% ORR) by iRECIST. This design yields a type I error rate of 0.0416 and a 
power of 0.8011 when the true ORR is 20%.  
3.5 Duration of Study  
The overall duration of the study is anticipated to be approximately 2 years fr om the first 
subject’s first visit until the last subject’s last visit. For regulatory reporting purposes, the end 
of the study is defined as the date the last subject completes their End of Therapy Visit or the 
last subject completes 6 months of therapy, whichever occurs first.

disease, signed informed consent).  
• Received < 44 of 56 eFT508 doses or less than 2 doses of avelumab in Cycle 1 for reasons  
other than the occurrence of a DLT or drug intolerability.  
• Received other antitumor treatments during Cycle 1 . 
The DLT Evaluable Population will be used in the determination of the MTD in Part 1.

5 STUDY ASSESSMENTS  
5.1 Efficacy Assessments  
5.1.1 Clinical Response Assessment  (RECIST)  
The treatment response for each subject will be determined at the timepoints in the Schedule 
of Events using RECIST v1.1 guidelines . At each assessment, subjects will be assigned a 
treatment response by the Investigator from among the following RECIST categories : 
 Comple te Response (CR)  
 Partial Response (PR)  
 Stable Disease (SD)  
 Prog essive Disease (PD)  
 Not Evaluable (NE)  
Definitions for the above categories are described  in Protocol Section 13.6.4.  
5.1.2 Clinical Response Assessment (iRECIST)  
The treatment response for each sub ject will also be determined at the timepoints in the 
Schedule of Events using iRECIST  guidelines. At each assessment, subjects will be assigned 
a treatment response by the Investigator from among the following iRECIST  categories:

6.2 Secondary Efficacy Endpoints  
6.2.1 Progression Free Survival  
PFS is defined as the interval from the start of study therapy until the first documentation of 
disease progression or death from any cause  (whichever occurs first) . Disease progression 
may be radiological progression  or clinical pr ogression. The start of study therapy is the first 
dose date of eFT508.  
Using RECIST v1.1, PFS will be calculated as:  
 PFS (days) = Date of PD  (radiological pro gression) , clinical progression,  or death 
(whichever occurs first ) – first dose date of eFT508 + 1 
Using iRECIST, iPFS will be calculated as:  
 iPFS (days) = Date of iU PD (radiological progression) , clinical progression,  or death 
(whichever occurs first ) – first dose date of eFT508 + 1  
Radiological progression will be based on the Investigator assess ment using RECIST 
v1.1/iRECIST.  
The date of clinical progression will be defined as the date of study discontinuation due to 
clinical progression in the absence of radiological progression.  

eFFECTOR Therapeutics Inc . 
eFT508 -0006  Statistical Analysis Plan                                                                                   Page 26 
 
To be counted as radiological disease progression  using iRECIST , the iUPD assessment must 
be confirmed as iCPD at the next iRECIST assessment. If an iUPD response is assessed but 
the p rogression is not confirmed (e.g . a later assessment of iSD, iPR, or iCR is recorded ) then 
the initial iUPD will not be counted as an eve nt for determination of iPFS.  To be as 
conservative as possible, in the the case that an assessment of iUPD is recorded and no 
subsequent  evaluable  iRECI ST assessments are recorded then the iUPD event will be treated 
as confirmed  in the calculation of iPF S. 
6.2.1.1  Censoring Rules for Progression Free Survival  
The following rules will be applied for both PFS and iPFS.  
Subjects who have n ot had radiological progression  (i.e. an assessment of PD using RECIST 
or a previous response of iUPD using iRECIST that is con firmed iCPD  at the next iRECIST 
assessment ), have not experienced clinical progression , or died at the time of analysis will be 
censored at the time of the last response assessment.  However, if the subject experiences 
progression (either clinical or radio logical) or dies after 2 or more missed visits, the subject 
will be censored at the time of the latest response  assessment  prior to the two missed visits . 
In addition, subjects that do not have documented disease progression but begin subsequent 
anticancer  therapy will be censored on the date the subsequent anticancer therapy is started.   
If the subject has no evaluable visits or does not have a baseline assessment, they will be 
censored at Day 1.  
The PFS time will be derived based on scan/assessment dates  and not visit dates  to determine 
radiological progression .  Assessments/scans contributing towards a particular visit may be 
performed on different dates.  The following rules will be applied  for this calculation : 
 Date of radiological progression will be determined based on the earliest of the 
assessment/scan dates of the component that triggered the progression  (PD using 
RECIST or iUPD using iRECIST that is confirmed iCPD at the next assessment) .   
 When censoring a subject for PFS , the subject will be cen sored at the latest of the 
assessment/scan dates contributing to a particular overall visit assessment.  
Overall visit assessments will be determined for each assessment (scheduled or unscheduled) 
and will contribute to the derivation of PFS .
