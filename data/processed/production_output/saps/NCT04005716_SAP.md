# Study Design

## Design Type
This is a Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study comparing tislelizumab (BGB-A317) plus platinum-based chemotherapy versus placebo plus platinum-based chemotherapy.

## Randomization
- **Randomization Ratio**: To be specified based on protocol
- **Randomization Method**: Interactive Web/Voice Response System (IWRS/IXRS)
- **Stratification Factors**: To be defined per protocol requirements

## Blinding
- **Blinded Parties**: Patients, investigators, study site personnel, sponsor study team, and data analysts will remain blinded to treatment assignment throughout the study
- **Blinding Method**: Matching placebo will be used to maintain the blind
- **Unblinding Procedures**: Emergency unblinding will be available through the IWRS/IXRS system when medically necessary for patient safety

## Treatment Arms

### Experimental Arm
**Tislelizumab + Platinum-Based Chemotherapy**
- Tislelizumab (BGB-A317) administered per protocol-specified dosing schedule
- Platinum-based chemotherapy (carboplatin or cisplatin) plus etoposide administered per standard of care

### Control Arm  
**Placebo + Platinum-Based Chemotherapy**
- Matching placebo administered per protocol-specified schedule
- Platinum-based chemotherapy (carboplatin or cisplatin) plus etoposide administered per standard of care

## Sample Size

**Sample Size Justification:**

The null hypothesis (H0) states that there is no difference in efficacy between the experimental treatment and placebo (treatment effect = 0). The alternative hypothesis (H1) states that there is a clinically meaningful difference between treatments.

Based on Phase 2 data and clinical considerations, the study is powered to detect a clinically relevant treatment effect with alpha = 0.05 (two-sided) and power = 80% (1-beta = 0.80).
